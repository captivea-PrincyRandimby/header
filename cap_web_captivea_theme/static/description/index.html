Captivea Theme

Brand theme for Odoo 19 Website — colour palette, typography, design tokens, a full library of branded content snippets and SEO-ready page templates.

Overview

Captivea Theme turns a standard Odoo 19 website into the Captivea brand universe. It ships the brand design system (colours, gradients, tints, shadows, radii, Nunito typography) together with a large set of ready-to-use content sections and complete page templates, so marketing pages can be assembled in minutes while staying consistent and SEO-friendly.

Key features

Brand design system

Colour palette & design tokens (signature gradient, +30%/+60% tints, brand shadow, section/card radii) and Nunito typography, exposed as CSS variables.

Branded snippets

30+ Captivea content sections (s_cap_*): hero header, context, solution, key features, pain points, gains, methodology, expertise, teams, sectors, offices, coverage, stats, FAQ, CTA and more.

SEO page templates

17 pre-built templates: Home, Sub-sector, Sector hub, Team, About, Country, Office, Odoo Partner, Product pillar, Odoo app, Captivea add-on, ISV partner, Comparison (ERP & CMS), Offer, Customer benefit, Case study — plus an auto-generated “Templates” menu.

Customer references

Server-rendered dynamic snippet listing published reference companies (logo wall), filterable in the editor by company, industry and tag.

Captivea Blog Posts

Dedicated dynamic blog snippet with editor Blog & Tag filters and a branded card (cover, category badge, teaser, auto-linked tags, author).

Comparison pages

Two comparison templates (ERP and CMS) with contenders, decision criteria, verdict and full cross-linking to every sibling comparison.

Branded components

Card & FAQ accordion styling, buttons per colour combination with gradient hover, eyebrow capsules, and smooth in-page anchor scrolling.

Integrations

Bundles the Captivea companion modules: Lucide icons, per-language logo, testimonials and JSON-LD SEO.
